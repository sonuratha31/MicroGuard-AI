MICROGUARD ANATOMY LAB

A closer look at the human body.

Drag to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Select a named structure for close-up, isolation, or hiding.

  • Normal: a front-facing cutaway with visible organs.
  • Exploded: separate system layers and major organ groups.
  • Organ focus: isolate and magnify the selected structure.
  • X-ray: artistic transparent layers, not a medical radiograph.
  • Search: find any named mesh, including tiny vessels and nerves.

Microplastic dots are synthetic, not measured. Model coverage follows the source atlas; it is not an exhaustive map of every tissue. Reproductive structures are excluded. Rotation changes the view; this model is not rigged for walking or joint posing.
