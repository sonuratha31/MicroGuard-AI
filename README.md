# MicroGuard AI

MicroGuard AI is an interactive microplastic exposure awareness platform that helps users understand how daily habits, plastic pollution, and environmental exposure may affect human health.

The project combines a personal exposure assessment, 3D human body explorer, global plastic pollution map, research insights, and a dashboard to make microplastic awareness simple, visual, and practical.

Problem It Solves
Microplastic pollution is becoming a serious environmental and public health concern, but most people do not know how daily habits may increase exposure.
MicroGuard AI helps users:
- Understand possible microplastic sources in daily life
- Learn how microplastics may enter the human body
- Explore global plastic pollution zones visually
- Check their personal exposure risk

Challenges We Faced
One major challenge was connecting 3D assets correctly after deployment. The project worked locally, but some textures and models showed 404 errors on GitHub Pages because the app was hosted under a custom repository path.
We solved this by updating the Vite configuration, fixing asset paths, testing the production build, resolving Git conflicts, and redeploying the project.
Another challenge was making the 3D map useful, not just decorative. We added pollution hotspots, colors, categories, and clickable marker information so users can understand pollution severity across different regions.
